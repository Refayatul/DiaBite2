# Product Context: Diabetes Demo App

## Why This Project Exists
This application addresses the growing need for effective diabetes management tools. Diabetes affects millions worldwide, requiring careful monitoring of blood glucose levels, dietary choices, and lifestyle factors. The app provides a comprehensive solution for diabetes patients to track their health metrics and make informed decisions.

## Problems It Solves
- **Blood Glucose Tracking**: Helps users monitor and log blood sugar levels
- **Dietary Management**: Enables tracking of food intake and nutritional information
- **Historical Analysis**: Provides insights into health trends over time
- **AI-Powered Suggestions**: Offers intelligent recommendations for better diabetes management
- **User Authentication**: Secure access to personal health data
- **Data Persistence**: Local caching and cloud synchronization

## How It Should Work
1. **User Onboarding**: Secure registration and login process
2. **Dashboard**: Central hub showing key health metrics and recent activity
3. **Food Search**: Comprehensive database for nutritional information
4. **Data Entry**: Easy logging of blood glucose readings and meals
5. **History & Analytics**: Visual representation of health trends
6. **AI Assistance**: Smart suggestions based on user data patterns
7. **Profile Management**: Personal settings and health information

## User Experience Goals
- **Intuitive Interface**: Clean, easy-to-navigate Jetpack Compose UI
- **Accessibility**: Designed for users of all ages and technical abilities
- **Performance**: Fast, responsive experience with offline capabilities
- **Privacy**: Secure handling of sensitive health data
- **Personalization**: Tailored recommendations and insights
- **Reliability**: Consistent functionality across different devices

## Target Users
- Type 1 and Type 2 diabetes patients
- Healthcare providers managing patient data
- Caregivers assisting diabetes patients
- Individuals interested in preventive health monitoring
